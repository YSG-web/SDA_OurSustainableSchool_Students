OUR SUSTAINABLE SCHOOL

What are we going to do?

In this project, you will follow these steps:

1. Observe your school and detect environmental problems  
2. Choose one problem and investigate it  
3. Plan your ideas  
4. Create an interactive infographic  
5. Prepare and record a video  
6. Present your work to the class
